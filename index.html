About

A camera in my bag, a country I hadn't been to yet, and a story I found once I got back to the timeline.

I'm Hao, a filmmaker and photographer in Boston. That instinct — finding the shape of something in the edit — is what I bring to client work too. I shoot and cut promos, event coverage, and social content for brands and small businesses. One person, start to finish: I plan it, shoot it, edit it, color it, and deliver it. Based in Boston, happy to travel — if you've got something in mind, let's talk.
